**Slogan: colimits model interconnection.** Electric circuits, chemical reaction networks, finite state automata and Markov processes are all described by **network diagrams**, whose wires are *undirected* — unlike the morphisms of a category. 7 Sketches Chapter 6 shows how universal constructions capture network compositionality: to install a light switch from open components (power source, switch, lamp + resistor), mark each component's **ports** (interface, a finite set — possibly empty), draw witnesses-to-connection $\circ$ pointing at two ports each, and *identify* the indicated ports. Formally this is a finite [[Colimit]] in $\mathbf{FinSet}$: the [[Coequalizer]] of the two functions $f, g : X \to Y$ from witnesses to ports is the set of terminals after interconnection (Example 6.42), and [[Pushout|pushouts]] glue components along shared ports.

> Sources: 7 Sketches §6.1 ("The ubiquity of network languages", "Connections via colimits", "Composition operations and wiring diagrams"), §6.2 (Examples 6.25, 6.42), §6.6; Kittenlab Lectures 9, 15.

Colimits "are a kind of epiphenomenon of the category"; to use them as an *operation* one packages them into the [[Cospan|category of cospans]] $\mathbf{Cospan}_{\mathcal{C}}$, whose composition and monoidal product *are* the colimits (a category with finite colimits is one with an [[Initial Object]] and all pushouts, Proposition 6.32). Network-style wiring diagrams are those of [[Hypergraph Category|hypergraph categories]], whose extra icons are **spiders** $s_{m,n} : m \to n$ that fuse when they share a leg — the [[Frobenius Monoid|Frobenius structure]]. Cospans in $\mathbf{FinSet}$ form "the theory of hypergraph categories" (Theorem 6.58), decorating cospans with data builds hypergraph categories of circuits ([[Decorated Cospan]]), and assembling all cospans into an [[Operad]] lets one tailor the compositional structure itself ([[Operad Algebra|$\mathbf{Cospan}$-algebras]] $=$ hypergraph props, Proposition 6.101).

The chapter completes an "informal hierarchy of compositional structures": [[Preorder|preorders]] → [[Category|categories]] → [[Monoidal Category|monoidal categories]] → [[Operad|operads]]; each with its own [[Wiring Diagram]] style ([[Discard and Copy Axioms|manufacturing]], [[Prop|signal flow]], [[Compact Closed Category|co-design]], networks).
