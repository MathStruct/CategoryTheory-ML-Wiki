**Exercise 5.77.** Behaviours of the reversed add icon $1 \to 2$ and reversed copy icon $2 \to 1$.

## Solution

$B(\text{add}^{\mathrm{op}}) = \{(x, (y, z)) \mid x = y + z\}$; $B(\text{copy}^{\mathrm{op}}) = \{((y, z), x) \mid x = y = z\}$. See [[Behavior of a Signal Flow Graph]].
