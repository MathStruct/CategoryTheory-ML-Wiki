**Exercise 1.69.** Choose sets $X, Y$ with at least three elements, a surjective non-identity $f : X \twoheadrightarrow Y$, two partitions $P, Q$ of $Y$, and compute $f^*(P), f^*(Q)$.

> See [[Pushforward and Pullback of Partitions]], Example 1.68.

## Solution

Let $X = \mathbb{Z}$, $Y = \{n, z, p\}$, $f$ sending negatives to $n$, zero to $z$, positives to $p$. With $P = (nz)(p)$ and $Q = (np)(z)$: $f^*(P) = \{\{x \leq 0\}, \{x \geq 1\}\}$ and $f^*(Q) = \{\{0\}, \{x \neq 0\}\}$.
