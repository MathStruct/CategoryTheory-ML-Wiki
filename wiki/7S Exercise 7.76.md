**Exercise 7.76.** In the [[Interval Domain]]: 1. Why is $[2, 6] \in o_{[0,8]}$? 2. Why is $[2, 6] \notin o_{[0,5]} \cup o_{[4,8]}$?

## Solution

1. $0 < 2 \leq 6 < 8$.
2. $[2,6] \in o_{[0,5]}$ would need $6 < 5$; $[2,6] \in o_{[4,8]}$ would need $4 < 2$. Neither holds.
