**Exercise 1.17.** For the partition $\{11,12\},\{13\},\{21\},\{22,23\}$ of $\{11,12,13,21,22,23\}$, write down every pair $(a,b)$ in the same part (there should be 10).

> 7 Sketches §1.2.1; see [[Partition]], [[Equivalence Relation]]. Hint: "two elements $a, b$" may be the same element.

## Solution

$(11,11), (11,12), (12,11), (12,12), (13,13), (21,21), (22,22), (22,23), (23,22), (23,23)$.
