**Exercise 2.3.1.** What does $(\mathrm{id}_a \circ -)$ do to arrows terminating in $a$? What does $(- \circ \mathrm{id}_a)$ do to arrows originating from $a$?

## Solution

Nothing: $\mathrm{id}_a \circ h = h$ and $k \circ \mathrm{id}_a = k$ by the identity laws. See [[Identity Function]], [[Category]].
