**Exercise 1.11.** Let $A := \{h, 1\}$, $B := \{1,2,3\}$. 1. Write the eight subsets of $B$. 2. Union of two nonempty subsets. 3. The six elements of $A \times B$. 4. The five elements of $A \sqcup B$. 5. Viewing $A, B \subseteq \{h,1,2,3\}$, the four elements of $A \cup B$.

> 7 Sketches §1.2.1; see [[Set]], [[Power Set]].

## Solution

1. $\varnothing, \{1\}, \{2\}, \{3\}, \{1,2\}, \{1,3\}, \{2,3\}, \{1,2,3\}$.
2. E.g. $\{1,2,3\} \cup \{1\} = \{1,2,3\}$.
3. $(h,1), (h,2), (h,3), (1,1), (1,2), (1,3)$.
4. $(h,1), (1,1), (1,2), (2,2), (3,2)$ (tagged by which set they come from).
5. $h, 1, 2, 3$.
