**Exercise 1.10.** Is it true that 1. $\mathbb{N} = \{n \in \mathbb{Z} \mid n \geq 0\}$? 2. $\mathbb{N} = \{n \in \mathbb{Z} \mid n \geq 1\}$? 3. $\varnothing = \{n \in \mathbb{Z} \mid 1 < n < 2\}$?

> 7 Sketches §1.2.1; see [[Set]].

## Solution

1. True. 2. False: $0 \in \mathbb{N}$ but $0 \notin \{n \geq 1\}$. 3. True: no integer lies strictly between $1$ and $2$.
