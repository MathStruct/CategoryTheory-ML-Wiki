**Exercise 2.68.** 1. Find another [[Monoidal Monotone Map]] $\mathbf{Cost} \to \mathbf{Bool}$. 2. Find a [[Lawvere Metric Space]] on which it and $f(x) = [x = 0]$ give different preorders.

## Solution

1. $u(x) = [x < \infty]$ from [[7S Exercise 2.44]].
2. Two points $A, B$ with $d(A,B) = d(B,A) = 5$: $\mathcal{X}_f$ is the [[Discrete Preorder]] on $\{A, B\}$, while $\mathcal{X}_u$ is the [[Codiscrete Preorder]] ($A \leq B \leq A$).
