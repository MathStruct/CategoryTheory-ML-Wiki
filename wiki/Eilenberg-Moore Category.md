A **monad algebra** (Eilenberg–Moore algebra) for a [[Monad]] $(T, \eta, \mu)$ is an [[Algebra of an Endofunctor|algebra]] $(a, \alpha : T a \to a)$ compatible with the monad: the **unit law** $\alpha \circ \eta_a = \mathrm{id}_a$ and the **multiplication law** $\alpha \circ \mu_a = \alpha \circ T \alpha$. The second says $\alpha$ is itself an algebra morphism $(T a, \mu_a) \to (a, \alpha)$. Monad algebras and algebra morphisms form the **Eilenberg–Moore category** $\mathcal{C}^T$.

```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzcd}
a \arrow[r, "\eta_a"] \arrow[dr, "\mathrm{id}_a"'] & T a \arrow[d, "\alpha"] & & T(T a) \arrow[r, "T \alpha"] \arrow[d, "\mu_a"'] & T a \arrow[d, "\alpha"] \\
 & a & & T a \arrow[r, "\alpha"'] & a
\end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
```

> Sources: DaoFP §15.5 ("Monad Algebras", "Eilenberg-Moore category", "Kleisli category"); §14.7 (the expression monad `Ex`); 7 Sketches §1.4.4 (fixed points of a [[Closure Operator]]); Kittenlab Lecture 5 (monoids as algebras).

**Intuition.** Monads generate expressions, algebras evaluate them; compatibility says evaluating a bare variable returns it (`alg . return = id`, ruling out `alg (Var c) = 'a'`) and evaluating a flattened nested expression equals evaluating inside first (`alg (join mma) = alg (fmap alg mma)`). For the free monoid monad, algebras are exactly monoids; for a closure operator on a preorder, algebras are its fixed points.

**Theorem (every monad comes from an adjunction).** Define $U^T : \mathcal{C}^T \to \mathcal{C}$, $(a, \alpha) \mapsto a$, and $F^T a := (T a, \mu_a)$ (a monad algebra by the monad laws), $F^T f = T f$ (an algebra morphism by naturality of $\mu$). Take unit $\eta$ and counit $\varepsilon_{(a, \alpha)} := \alpha$ (an algebra morphism $F^T U^T (a,\alpha) \to (a, \alpha)$ by the multiplication law); the triangle identities follow from the unit laws. Then $U^T F^T = T$ on objects and arrows, the unit agrees by construction, and $U^T \varepsilon F^T$ at $a$ is $\varepsilon_{(T a, \mu_a)} = \mu_a$. So $T$ is generated by $F^T \dashv U^T$ ([[Monads from Adjunctions]]). $\blacksquare$

The [[Kleisli Category]] is the full subcategory of *free* algebras $(T a, \mu_a)$; among adjunctions generating $T$ (a 2-category), Kleisli is initial and Eilenberg–Moore terminal.

````tabs
# monad algebras for the list monad are monoids: α : [m] -> m must satisfy the two laws
α_sum(xs) = sum(xs; init=0)                       # (Int, +, 0)
α_sum([5]) == 5                                   # unit law: α ∘ η = id
xss = [[1, 2], [3]]
α_sum(reduce(vcat, xss)) == α_sum(map(α_sum, xss))  # multiplication law: α ∘ μ = α ∘ T α
# a non-example: α(xs) = length(xs) fails the unit law (α([5]) = 1 ≠ 5)
```

-- an algebra for the expression monad Ex compatible with return and join
data Ex x = Val Int | Var x | Plus (Ex x) (Ex x) deriving Functor
algChar :: Ex Char -> Char
algChar (Val n)      = toEnum n
algChar (Var c)      = c                          -- alg . return = id
algChar (Plus e1 e2) = maximum [algChar e1, algChar e2]
-- monad algebras for [] are Monoids: alg = mconcat
````
