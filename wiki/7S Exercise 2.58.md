**Exercise 2.58.** Fill in the distance table for the [[Weighted Graph]] $X$ of Eq. (2.56) ($A \xrightarrow{3} C$, $B \xrightarrow{2} A$, $B \xrightarrow{5} D$, $C \xrightarrow{3} B$, $D \xrightarrow{6} C$).

## Solution

| $d_X$ | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0 | 6 | 3 | 11 |
| B | 2 | 0 | 5 | 5 |
| C | 5 | 3 | 0 | 8 |
| D | 11 | 9 | 6 | 0 |

E.g. $d(A, B) = 3 + 3$ via $C$; $d(A, D) = 3 + 3 + 5$.
