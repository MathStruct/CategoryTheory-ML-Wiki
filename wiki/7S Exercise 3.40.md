**Exercise 3.40.** Give two functors $F, G : (a \xrightarrow{f} b) \to (a' \underset{f_2}{\overset{f_1}{\rightrightarrows}} b')$ that agree on objects but differ on morphisms.

## Solution

$F(a) = G(a) = a'$, $F(b) = G(b) = b'$, $F(f) = f_1$, $G(f) = f_2$. Functors are not in general determined by their action on objects.
