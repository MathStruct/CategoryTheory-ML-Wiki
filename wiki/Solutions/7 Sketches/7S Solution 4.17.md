**Solution to [[7S Exercise 4.17|Exercise 4.17]].**

$M_X^3 = d_X$ (the distance matrix of [[7S Exercise 2.58]]), $M_Y^2 = d_Y$; $d_X \ast M_\Phi$ has columns $(17, 11, 14, 20)$, $(20, 14, 17, 9)$, $(\infty, \infty, \infty, \infty)$, and multiplying by $d_Y$ gives exactly the matrix of Exercise 4.15. They agree. See [[Matrix Multiplication in a Quantale]].
