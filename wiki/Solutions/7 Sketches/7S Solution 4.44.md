**Solution to [[7S Exercise 4.44|Exercise 4.44]].**

The union of the two weighted graphs $X$ (on $A, B, C, D$) and $Y$ (on $x, y, z$) together with the bridges $B \xrightarrow{11} x$ and $D \xrightarrow{9} y$ as extra weighted edges.
