**Solution to [[7S Exercise 1.114|Exercise 1.114]].**

| $p$ | $q$ | $f(p) \leq q$ | $p \leq g(q)$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | yes | yes |
| 1 | 2 | no | no |
| 1 | 4 | yes | yes |
| 2 | 1 | no | no |
| 2 | 2 | yes | yes |
| 2 | 4 | yes | yes |
| 3.9 | 1 | no | no |
| 3.9 | 2 | no | no |
| 3.9 | 4 | yes | yes |
| 4 | 1 | no | no |
| 4 | 2 | no | no |
| 4 | 4 | yes | yes |

All agree, so $f \dashv g$; yet $g$ does not preserve joins ([[Right Adjoints Preserve Meets]] but not joins).

> Sources: 7 Sketches, Exercise 1.114 and Solution A.1.
