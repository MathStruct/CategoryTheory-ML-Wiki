**Solution to [[7S Exercise 5.41|Exercise 5.41]].**

1. The identity matrix ($1$ on the diagonal, $0$ elsewhere). 2. $n = 2$: $A = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ give $(AB)(1,1) = 1$ but $(BA)(1,1) = 0$.
