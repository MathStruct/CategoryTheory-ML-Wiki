**Solution to [[7S Exercise 5.21|Exercise 5.21]].**

1. Yes, since $R(x,y)$ implies $x \leq_P y$. 2. No: $Q = \{1\}$, $P = \{1\}$ with $R = \varnothing$; $g$ is monotone ($\leq_P$ is reflexive) but $(g(1), g(1)) \notin R$.
