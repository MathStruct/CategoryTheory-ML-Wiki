**Exercise 3.17.** List the morphisms of the square with a diagonal $j : A \to D$ and equations $f \mathbin{;} h = j = g \mathbin{;} i$.

## Solution

$A, A\mathbin{;}f, A\mathbin{;}g, A\mathbin{;}j, B, B\mathbin{;}h, C, C\mathbin{;}i, D$ — nine, since $f \mathbin{;} h = j = g \mathbin{;} i$.
