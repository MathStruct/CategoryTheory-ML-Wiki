#definition #example

A [[Preorder]] $(P, \leq)$ is a **dagger preorder** if the [[Identity Function]] $\mathrm{id}_P$ is a [[Monotone Map]] $(P, \leq) \to (P, \leq^{\mathrm{op}})$ — that is, for all $p, q$: $p \leq q$ implies $q \leq p$. The name comes from linear algebra ([[Dagger Category|dagger categories]]).

> Sources: 7 Sketches Example 1.72, Exercise 1.73, Remark 1.74.

In a dagger preorder $\leq$ is symmetric, reflexive and transitive: dagger preorders are exactly [[Equivalence Relation|equivalence relations]]. A *skeletal* dagger preorder (one that is also a [[Partial Order]]) is a [[Discrete Preorder]] and hence "can be identified with" its underlying [[Set]] ([[7S Exercise 1.73]]).

Remark 1.74 explains "can be identified with": any $A$ gives a unique $B$ and vice versa, with both round-trips returning where they started — the notion later made precise as [[Isomorphism]] of the corresponding objects, or [[Equivalence of Categories]]. Every [[Codiscrete Preorder]] is a dagger preorder; as categories, dagger preorders are thin [[Groupoid|groupoids]].
