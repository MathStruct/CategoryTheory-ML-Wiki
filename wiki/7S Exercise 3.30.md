**Exercise 3.30.** For $f : \{a,b,c\} \to \underline{3}$, $f(a) = 2, f(b) = 1, f(c) = 3$: what is $f^{-1}$, and how many isomorphisms $\{a,b,c\} \to \underline{3}$ are there?

## Solution

$f^{-1}(1) = b$, $f^{-1}(2) = a$, $f^{-1}(3) = c$. There are $3! = 6$ isomorphisms; in general $n!$ between two $n$-element sets. See [[Isomorphism]].
