#definition #example

From every [[Set]] $X$ we may construct its **codiscrete preorder** $(X, \leq)$ by equipping it with the total relation $X \times X \subseteq X \times X$: for all $x, y$ we have $x \leq y$ (and hence $y \leq x$). Its [[Partial Order|poset]] reflection is the one-element poset.

> Sources: 7 Sketches Example 1.33; Kittenlab Lecture 5.

The construction is a functor $\mathbf{Set} \to \mathbf{Preord}$, right adjoint to the underlying-set functor (compare the [[Discrete Preorder]], which is the left adjoint). As a category, a codiscrete preorder is the [[Codiscrete Category]] (an "indiscrete" [[Groupoid]] in which every hom-set is a singleton), and it is a [[Dagger Preorder]].
