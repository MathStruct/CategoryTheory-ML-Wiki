**Exercise 9.8.2.** The singleton functor $F(c) = \{c\}$ (on arrows, the unique map between singletons): show $F$ representable iff $\mathcal{C}$ has an [[Initial Object]].

## Solution

$F \cong \mathcal{C}(a, -)$ means each $\mathcal{C}(a, c)$ is a singleton, i.e. $a$ is initial; conversely if $0$ is initial, $\mathcal{C}(0, -) \cong F$ naturally.
