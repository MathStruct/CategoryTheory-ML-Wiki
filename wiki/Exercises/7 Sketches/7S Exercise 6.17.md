**Exercise 6.17.** Let $f : A \to C$, $g : B \to C$, $h : C \to D$ in a category with [[Coproduct]]s. Show
1. $\iota_A \mathbin{;} [f, g] = f$;
2. $\iota_B \mathbin{;} [f, g] = g$;
3. $[f, g] \mathbin{;} h = [f \mathbin{;} h, g \mathbin{;} h]$;
4. $[\iota_A, \iota_B] = \mathrm{id}_{A + B}$.
