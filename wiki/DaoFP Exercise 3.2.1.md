**Exercise 3.2.1.** Show that both sides of the naturality condition $(- \circ f^{-1}) \circ (g \circ -) = (g \circ -) \circ (- \circ f^{-1})$, acting on $h : a \to x$, reduce to $g \circ h \circ f^{-1} : b \to y$.

## Solution

Left: $(g \circ h) \circ f^{-1}$; right: $g \circ (h \circ f^{-1})$; equal by associativity — the naturality is automatic here. See [[Isomorphism]], [[Natural Transformation]].
