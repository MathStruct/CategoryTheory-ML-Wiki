**Exercise 2.8.** Is it really easy to check that a commutative [[Monoid]] $(M, \ast, e)$ makes $(\mathrm{Disc}_M, =, e, \ast)$ a [[Symmetric Monoidal Preorder]]?

## Solution

Yes. Condition (a) says $x_1 = y_1, x_2 = y_2 \Rightarrow x_1 \ast x_2 = y_1 \ast y_2$, a tautology; (b) and (c) are the monoid equations; (d) is commutativity.
