**Exercise 5.24.** The [[Free Monoid]] on $A$ is the free category on the one-vertex graph $\mathrm{Loop}(A)$. 1. Elements for $A = \{a\}$? 2. A well-known monoid isomorphic to it? 3. Elements for $A = \{a, b\}$?

## Solution

1. $a^0, a^1, a^2, \dots$ with $a^i \ast a^j = a^{i+j}$. 2. $(\mathbb{N}, +, 0)$ via $a^i \mapsto i$. 3. Words in $a$ and $b$: $[\,], [a], [b], [a,a], [a,b], \dots$
