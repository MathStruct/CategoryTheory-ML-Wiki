#paper

**Mathematical Foundations for a Compositional Account of the Bayesian Brain** — Toby St Clere Smithe (2022). [arXiv:2212.12538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12538) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.12538v3)).

Doctoral thesis supplying functorial semantics for approximate inference: Bayesian lenses, fibrations of statistical games whose sections classify inference problems (relative entropy as a strict section; maximum likelihood and free energy as lax sections), copy-composition by coparameterization, and a general formalisation of open dynamical systems via polynomial functors.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:2212.12538v3, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- §5.2.1: copy-composition by coparameterization ($\mathbf{Copara}_2$)
- §5.2.3–5.2.4: coparameterized Bayesian lenses compose optically
- Fibrations of statistical games and their (lax) sections
