#paper

**Algebraic Databases** — Patrick Schultz, David I. Spivak, Christina Vasilakopoulou & Ryan Wisnesky (2016; Theory Appl. Categ. 32 (2017)). [arXiv:1602.03501](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03501) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.03501v3)).

A categorical model of databases with an algebraic type side: schemas pair an entity category with an observables profunctor into a multi-sorted algebraic theory, instances are functors whose type part is an algebra, and schema mappings induce the adjoint data-migration functors. Schemas, mappings, instances and queries are organised into one proarrow equipment.
