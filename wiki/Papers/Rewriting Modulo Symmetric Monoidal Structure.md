#paper

**Rewriting modulo symmetric monoidal structure** — Filippo Bonchi, Fabio Gadducci, Aleks Kissinger, Pawel Sobocinski & Fabio Zanasi (2016; LICS 2016). [arXiv:1602.06771](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.06771) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.06771v2)).

Shows that rewriting string diagrams — morphisms of free symmetric monoidal categories — can be done by double-pushout rewriting of hypergraphs. With Frobenius structure the correspondence is exact; without it one must restrict to convex matches and convex DPO steps. Establishes hypergraph rewriting as a sound and complete implementation of equational reasoning in symmetric monoidal theories.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1602.06771v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Definition 3.1: hypergraphs
- Definition 3.2, Theorem 3.3: Frobenius termgraphs $\mathbf{FTerm}_\Sigma \cong \mathbf S_\Sigma + \mathbf{Frob}$
- Definition 4.1: DPO rewriting with discrete interfaces
- Theorems 4.4, 4.6: DPO rewriting of termgraphs coincides with rewriting in $\mathbf S_\Sigma + \mathbf{Frob}$
- Proposition 4.7: the category of hypergraphs is adhesive
- Definitions 5.4–5.5, Theorem 5.6: convex matches and convex DPO for plain symmetric monoidal theories
