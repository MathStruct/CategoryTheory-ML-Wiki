**Representable Markov Categories and Comparison of Statistical Experiments in Categorical Probability** — Tobias Fritz, Tomáš Gonda, Paolo Perrone & Eigil Fjeldgren Rischel (2020). [arXiv:2010.07416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07416) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.07416v3)); Theoretical Computer Science 961, 113896 (2023).

Defines representable Markov categories (where kernels correspond to deterministic maps into distribution objects), characterises second-order stochastic dominance, and proves a synthetic Blackwell–Sherman–Stein theorem on comparing statistical experiments.
