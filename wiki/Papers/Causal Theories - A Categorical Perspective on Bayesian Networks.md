**Causal Theories: A Categorical Perspective on Bayesian Networks** — Brendan Fong (2013). [arXiv:1301.6201](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6201) (v1, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.6201v1)).

Master's dissertation proposing causal theories — symmetric monoidal categories whose objects are variables and whose morphisms deduce information about one variable from another — as an algebraic framework for Bayesian networks and causal reasoning, with graphical representations matching information flow.
