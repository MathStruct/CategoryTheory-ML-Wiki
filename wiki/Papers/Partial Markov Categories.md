#paper

**Partial Markov Categories** — Elena Di Lavore, Mario Román & Paweł Sobociński (2025). [arXiv:2502.03477](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03477) (v4, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.03477v4)).

Introduces partial Markov categories — copy-discard categories with conditionals — as a setting for probabilistic reasoning with failure and exact observations; proves that the Kleisli category of the Maybe monad over a Markov category is one, develops a synthetic normalisation, compares Pearl's and Jeffrey's update rules, and proves a synthetic Bayes theorem.
