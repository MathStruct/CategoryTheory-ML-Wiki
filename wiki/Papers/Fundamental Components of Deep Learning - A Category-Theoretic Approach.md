**Fundamental Components of Deep Learning: A category-theoretic approach** — Bruno Gavranović (2024). [arXiv:2403.13001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13001) (v1, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.13001v1)).

Doctoral thesis developing an end-to-end, uniform and prescriptive categorical foundation for deep learning, using actegories, Para, optics and lenses to describe backpropagation, architectures and learning.
