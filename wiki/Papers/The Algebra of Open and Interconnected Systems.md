**The Algebra of Open and Interconnected Systems** — Brendan Fong (2016). [arXiv:1609.05382](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05382) (v1, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.05382v1)).

Doctoral thesis introducing hypergraph categories and decorated cospans as the algebra of network-style diagrammatic languages — circuits, signal flow graphs, Markov processes, Petri nets — with semantics given by hypergraph functors.
