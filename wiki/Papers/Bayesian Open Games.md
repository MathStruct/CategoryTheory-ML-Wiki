#paper

**Bayesian open games** — Joe Bolt, Jules Hedges & Philipp Zahn (2019). [arXiv:1910.03656](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.03656) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.03656v2)); Compositionality, Volume 5 (2023) (October 4, 2023) compositionality:13528.

Generalises open games to stochastic environments, stochastic choices by players and incomplete information, using Markov kernels, so that Bayesian updating appears in the backward pass of a game.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1910.03656v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].
