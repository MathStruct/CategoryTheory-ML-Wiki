#paper

**Generalized Lens Categories via functors $\mathcal{C}^{\rm op}\to\mathsf{Cat}$** — David I. Spivak (2019). [arXiv:1908.02202](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02202) (v4, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.02202v4)).

Observes that the Grothendieck construction of the fibrewise opposite of an indexed category $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Cat}$ yields a category of generalised lenses, recovering ordinary lenses from the simple fibration and giving a uniform source of bidirectional morphisms (including those of polynomial functors).
