#paper

**Regular and relational categories: Revisiting 'Cartesian bicategories I'** — Brendan Fong & David I Spivak (2019). [arXiv:1909.00069](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.00069) (v1, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.00069v1)).

Gives a direct axiomatisation of bicategories of relations — relational po-categories — and proves that the 2-category of regular categories is equivalent to that of relational po-categories. Regular logic ($=, \top, \wedge, \exists$) is the internal logic of regular categories and exactly the logic needed to compose binary relations; the paper emphasises the graphical calculus throughout.
