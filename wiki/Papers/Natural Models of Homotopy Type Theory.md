#paper

**Natural models of homotopy type theory** — Steve Awodey (2014; Math. Struct. Comp. Sci. (2016)). [arXiv:1406.3219](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.3219) (v4, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.3219v4)).

Reformulates categories with families as representable natural transformations of presheaves, and expresses the type formers of dependent type theory as operations on such a map, using the associated polynomial functor. Gives natural models of extensional and of homotopy type theory.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1406.3219v4, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Definition 1: representable natural transformation of presheaves
- Proposition 2: a representable map $p : \dot{\mathcal U} \to \mathcal U$ is the same as a cwf
- Theorem 16: natural models of extensional Martin-Löf type theory with $\Pi$, $\Sigma$ and identity types
