#paper

**Bayesian Updates Compose Optically** — Toby St. Clere Smithe (2020). [arXiv:2006.01631](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01631) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01631v2)).

Shows that Bayesian inversion composes like the backward pass of a lens: inversions are *state-dependent* morphisms, organised as Grothendieck lenses for a state-indexed category, equivalently as [[Optic|optics]] for an action on presheaves. The main theorem is that the Bayesian inverse of a composite channel is the lens composite of the inverses, so exact inversion embeds channels functorially into Bayesian lenses.
