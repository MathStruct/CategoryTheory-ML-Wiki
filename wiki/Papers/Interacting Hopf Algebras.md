#paper

**Interacting Hopf Algebras** — Filippo Bonchi, Pawel Sobocinski & Fabio Zanasi (2014). [arXiv:1403.7048](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7048) (v4, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.7048v4)).

Introduces the theory IH of interacting Hopf algebras over a principal ideal domain — two Hopf algebra and two Frobenius algebra structures — and proves that it presents the PROP of linear relations over the field of fractions.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1403.7048v4, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- IH is isomorphic to the PROP of linear relations
