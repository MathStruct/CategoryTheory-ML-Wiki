#paper

**A Category for unifying Gaussian Probability and Nondeterminism** — Dario Stein & Richard Samuelson (2022). [arXiv:2204.14024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14024) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.14024v2)).

Introduces extended Gaussian distributions (a nondeterministic subspace plus a Gaussian on the quotient) and the Markov category GaussEx, proves it has conditionals, and obtains Gaussian relations as a hypergraph category into which Gaussian-decorated cospans map — unifying Gaussian noise, linear constraints and uninformative priors, after Willems' open stochastic systems.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:2204.14024v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Definition 3: extended Gaussian distribution
- Definitions 8, 11: extended Gaussian maps, GaussEx
- Theorem 13: GaussEx has conditionals
- Theorem 14: a hypergraph functor from Gaussian-decorated cospans to Gaussian relations
