**Actegories for the Working Amthematician** — Matteo Capucci & Bruno Gavranović (2022). [arXiv:2203.16351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16351) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.16351v3)).

A reference on actions of monoidal categories on categories (actegories): basic definitions and results, biactegories, tensor products and the hom-tensor adjunction, and monoidal, braided and symmetric monoidal actegories — the structure underlying Para and mixed optics.
