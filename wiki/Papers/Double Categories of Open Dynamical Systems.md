#paper

**Double Categories of Open Dynamical Systems (Extended Abstract)** — David Jaz Myers (2020). [arXiv:2005.05956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.05956) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.05956v2)); EPTCS 333, 2021, pp. 154-167.

Puts forward a general definition of open dynamical system with two kinds of morphism — covariant ones (trajectories, steady states, periodic orbits) and contravariant ones plugging variables of one system into parameters of another — organised in double categories.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:2005.05956v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Covariant and contravariant morphisms of open systems; double categories of systems
