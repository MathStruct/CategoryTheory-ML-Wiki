#paper

**Lenses and Learners** — Brendan Fong & Michael Johnson (2019). [arXiv:1903.03671](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.03671) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.03671v2)); In: J. Cheney, H-S. Ko (eds.): Proceedings of the Eighth International Workshop on Bidirectional Transformations (Bx 2019), Philadelphia, PA, USA, June 4, 2019, published at http://ceur-ws.org.

Shows that learners and lenses are tightly linked: there is a faithful identity-on-objects symmetric monoidal functor embedding asymmetric lenses into learners, and another embedding learners into symmetric lenses.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1903.03671v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Embeddings of asymmetric lenses into learners and of learners into symmetric lenses
