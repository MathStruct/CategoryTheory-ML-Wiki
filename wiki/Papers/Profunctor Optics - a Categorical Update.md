**Profunctor Optics, a Categorical Update** — Bryce Clarke, Derek Elkins, Jeremy Gibbons, Fosco Loregian, Bartosz Milewski, Emily Pillmore & Mario Román (2020). [arXiv:2001.07488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.07488) (v6, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.07488v6)); Compositionality, Volume 6 (2024) (February 23, 2024) compositionality:13530.

Generalises Pastro–Street Tambara theory to mixed, enriched optics, describing mixed $\mathcal V$-enriched profunctor optics with category structure, and catalogues optics (lenses, prisms, traversals, grates, Kleisli optics…) as instances.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:2001.07488v6, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Mixed optics; Tambara representation theorem for enriched mixed optics
