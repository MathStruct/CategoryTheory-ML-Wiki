**Cartesian differential categories revisited** — G. S. H. Cruttwell (2012). [arXiv:1208.4070](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.4070) (v1, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.4070v1)).

Revisits the definition of Cartesian differential categories with a slightly more general version, showing such categories are comonadic over Cartesian categories and treating restriction structure.
