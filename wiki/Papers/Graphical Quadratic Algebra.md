#paper

**Graphical Quadratic Algebra** — Dario Stein, Fabio Zanasi, Robin Piedeleu & Richard Samuelson (2024). [arXiv:2403.02284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02284) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.02284v3)).

Gives a complete diagrammatic axiomatisation of quadratic relations, extending the graphical algebra of linear relations with a probabilistic (Gaussian) layer, so that noise, exact constraints and uninformative priors are all string diagrams with sound and complete equations.
