#paper

**Polynomial Functors: A Mathematical Theory of Interaction** — Nelson Niu & David I. Spivak (2023). [arXiv:2312.00990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00990) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00990v2)).

A book-length treatment of the category $\mathbf{Poly}$ of polynomial functors on Set and dependent lenses between them, as a theory of interactive systems: positions are what a system shows, directions what it accepts. Covers the monoidal structures of Poly, dynamical systems and Moore machines as lenses, and polynomial comonoids as categories.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:2312.00990v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Definition 2.1: polynomial functor $\sum_i y^{p[i]}$
- Definition 2.8: positions and directions
- Definition 3.1: dependent lens; the category $\mathbf{Poly}$
- Definition 3.65: parallel product
- Definition 4.1: Moore machines as lenses
