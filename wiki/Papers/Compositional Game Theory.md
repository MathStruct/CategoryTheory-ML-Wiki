#paper

**Compositional game theory** — Neil Ghani, Jules Hedges, Viktor Winschel & Philipp Zahn (2016). [arXiv:1603.04641](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04641) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.04641v3)).

Introduces open games: games with a play function, a coplay function returning utilities to the past, and a best-response relation relative to a context. Open games compose sequentially and in parallel, forming a symmetric monoidal category whose string diagrams describe games built from parts, with Nash equilibria characterised compositionally.
