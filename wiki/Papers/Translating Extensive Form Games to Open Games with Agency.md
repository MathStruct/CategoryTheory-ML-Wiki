#paper

**Translating Extensive Form Games to Open Games with Agency** — Matteo Capucci, Neil Ghani, Jérémy Ledent & Fredrik Nordvall Forsberg (2021). [arXiv:2105.06763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06763) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06763v2)); EPTCS 372, 2022, pp. 221-234.

Shows that open games cover extensive-form games with perfect and imperfect information by adding a notion of player and agency, from which choice operators are constructed.
