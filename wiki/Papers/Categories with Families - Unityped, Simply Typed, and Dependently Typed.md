**Categories with Families: Unityped, Simply Typed, and Dependently Typed** — Simon Castellan, Pierre Clairambault & Peter Dybjer (2019). [arXiv:1904.00827](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00827) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.00827v2)).

A systematic account of categories with families as models of type theory at three levels. Unityped cwfs correspond to cartesian operads and Lawvere theories; simply typed ones to cartesian (closed) categories; dependently typed democratic ones with $\Sigma$ and extensional identity types to finitely complete and, with $\Pi$, locally cartesian closed categories. Constructs initial cwfs from syntax and shows equality in the free LCCC is undecidable.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1904.00827v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Definition 1: category with families
- Definitions 2–3: contextuality, democracy
- Definition 4: cwf morphisms
- Definitions 5–7, Theorems 1–2: unityped cwfs ≃ cartesian operads ≃ Lawvere theories
- Theorems 3–5: simply typed cwfs ≃ cartesian categories; with λβη, CCCs
- Theorems 6–7, 10: term models are (bi-)initial
- Theorems 8–9: democratic cwfs with $\Sigma$, $I_{\mathrm{ext}}$ (and $\Pi$) ≃ finitely complete categories (LCCCs)
- Theorems 11–12: the free LCCC on one object; its equality is undecidable
