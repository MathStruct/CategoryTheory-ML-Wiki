#paper

**Change Actions: Models of Generalised Differentiation** — Mario Alvarez-Picallo & C. -H. Luke Ong (2019; FoSSaCS 2019). [arXiv:1902.05465](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05465) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.05465v3)).

Develops the category theory of change actions: categories of change actions and differential maps, internal change actions in a cartesian category, and change action models as coalgebras of a copointed endofunctor, with tangent bundle functors. Shows that generalised cartesian differential categories give change action models, as do polynomials over Kleene algebras — whose derivatives are not additive, so change actions strictly generalise differential categories.
