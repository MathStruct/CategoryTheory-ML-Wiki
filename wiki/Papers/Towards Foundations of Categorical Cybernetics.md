#paper

**Towards Foundations of Categorical Cybernetics** — Matteo Capucci, Bruno Gavranović, Jules Hedges & Eigil Fjeldgren Rischel (2021). [arXiv:2105.06332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06332) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06332v2)); EPTCS 372, 2022, pp. 235-248.

Proposes a common categorical foundation for systems that act and learn: parametrised morphisms ($\mathbf{Para}$ over an actegory) combined with optics, so that gradient-based learners, Bayesian agents and open games are all parametrised optics differing in the backward structure; open games add a selection functor for best responses.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:2105.06332v2, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Definition 1: actegory; Definition 2: $\mathbf{Para}_\bullet(\mathcal C)$; Remarks 3–4: reparametrisation, CoPara
- Proposition 5: Para is a monad on actegories
- Definition 8: optics
- §4: open games as parametrised optics with selection functions
