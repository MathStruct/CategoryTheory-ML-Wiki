**Categories of Optics** — Mitchell Riley (2018). [arXiv:1809.00738](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00738) (v2, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.00738v2)).

A careful account of the general optic construction for a symmetric monoidal category, showing it is functorial and freely adds counits, and giving a general notion of lawfulness equivalent to the folklore profunctor-optic laws.
