#paper

**Structured Cospans** — John C. Baez & Kenny Courser (2019). [arXiv:1911.04630](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.04630) (v3, [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.04630v3)); Theory and Applications of Categories, Vol. 35, 2020, No. 48, pp 1771-1822.

Introduces structured cospans $L(a) \to x \leftarrow L(b)$ for a left adjoint $L$, obtaining hypergraph categories of open networks, which arise from symmetric monoidal double categories, and explains how they avoid technical problems of decorated cospans.

> Sources: the paper, arXiv:1911.04630v3, checked against the arXiv listing. Index: [[Papers]].

## Key definitions and results

- Structured cospan categories are hypergraph categories
- Symmetric monoidal double categories of structured cospans
