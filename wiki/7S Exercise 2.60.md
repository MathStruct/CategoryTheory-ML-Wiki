**Exercise 2.60.** Fill out the graph matrix $M_X$ for the graph $X$ of Eq. (2.56).

## Solution

$$M_X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & \infty & 3 & \infty \\ 2 & 0 & \infty & 5 \\ \infty & 3 & 0 & \infty \\ \infty & \infty & 6 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$ (rows/columns $A, B, C, D$). Its powers give $d_X$ ([[7S Exercise 2.105]]).
